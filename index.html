MPU-6050 no_std Rust Driver

Published crate
crates.io →Repository →
Problem

A no_std crate targets an RTOS with no heap and no OS — and Rust’s test runner needs std. Nothing in the firmware could be tested at all without the board on the desk.

What I did

Wrote the I2C driver register by register from the InvenSense datasheet — 1024-byte FIFO, config-derived packet sizing, overflow recovery — then built a mock I2C bus with transaction logging and five injectable fault modes.

How it was checked

48 tests at 91% line coverage, with NACK, bus-stuck, corrupt-byte, wrong-device and FIFO-overflow faults injected deliberately. Verified on silicon against a logic-analyser capture at 12 KB flash.

Built with
Rustembedded-hal 1.0I2CTI TM4C123Gcrates.io

PPG Blood Glucose Estimation

Edge ML
Problem

Blood glucose measurement means a finger prick. A photoplethysmography signal is non-invasive and cheap — but only useful if the error is small enough that a clinician would act on it.

What I did

Built a dual-stream model over STFT and DWT features, pairing graph convolutions with cross-attention Transformers, then quantised the 1.16M-parameter network for on-device inference.

How it was checked

1.973 mg/dL MAE · R² 0.989 across 6,727 segments from 217 subjects, with 96.1% of predictions inside the clinically acceptable ±15 mg/dL band.

Runs on

2.3 MB at FP16 and 1.2 MB at INT8 — 76 ms and 52 ms inference latency respectively.

Data forensics
Repository →
Problem

A collections team reported recovery up 11% month on month. Before anyone funds whatever caused it, somebody has to check whether the number is real.

What I did

Rebuilt recovery from 17 raw CSVs as a reproducible pipeline — profiling, dedup and reversal rules, an as-of joined golden dataset — then wrote the same logic as production-shaped SQL, with data-quality assertions that gate publication.

How it was checked

The rise was the calendar: a 31-day March beside a 28-day February accounts for 10.7 of the 11.0 points. Net recovery was flat at ₹0.555 Cr/day (CV 2.0%, trend p = 0.11), and duplicates and reversals counted as cash overstated the total by ₹12.06 Cr · 9.9%.

Prahar

Razorpay AI Buildathon
Repository →
Problem

A failed recurring debit in India gets a hard-capped number of NPCI retry attempts. Retry at the wrong hour and the attempt is spent on an account that was never going to have money in it.

What I did

Built an agent that decides whether, when and how to retry — a hazard model of when the payer is actually likely to be funded, run against the cap on attempts rather than a fixed schedule.

How it was checked

Evaluated by simulation with per-component ablations, and the write-up reports the metric that regressed next to the ones that improved.
